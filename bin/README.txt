Windows x64: install-windows.ps1 automatically downloads a pinned FFmpeg build
if no ffmpeg.exe is found here or on PATH. You can alternatively place your
own FFmpeg executable at bin/ffmpeg.exe. FFmpeg is separate third-party
software; its license is provided by the selected build's distributor.

Pinned download: https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static/releases/tag/b6.1.1
